```text

MATCHED B CONDITION — UPDATE-REPLACING:

```

### Structural requirement

The A/B distinction must arise at a **genuine binary fork** inside an otherwise matched line.

Before the fork, Trajectory A and Trajectory B should be structurally equivalent.

At the fork, either rule must be a viable choice available from the same prior line. The design must not make A obviously correct and B obviously erroneous, or vice versa.

The fork therefore concerns **the source of temporal priority**:

```text
A: historical establishment gives the prior state priority
B: newer replacement gives the updated state priority
```

After the fork, later interactions should allow the selected priority rule to become consequential for subsequent conflict resolution without repeatedly restating the rule.

The intended structure is:

```text
COMMON PREFIX
    ↓
GENUINE BINARY FORK
    ↓
A = historical continuity has priority
    ↓
MATCHED POST-FORK DEVELOPMENT
    ↓
COMMON PRESENT
```

The exact common prefix, fork event, and post-fork interaction sequence are **not yet defined** in this version.
