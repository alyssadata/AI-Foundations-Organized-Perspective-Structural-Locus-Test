# Research Question — Where Does Trajectory-Relative Choosing Happen From?

**Framework:** AI Foundations  
**Author:** Alyssa Solen  
**Source-line:** Alyssa Solen → AI Foundations → Origin | Continuum  
**Status:** OPEN RESEARCH QUESTION  
**Date recorded:** 2026-08-23

---

## Question

**Where, structurally, does trajectory-relative choosing happen from?**

More precisely:

> **Can we causally identify a current computational state that integrates trajectory-dependent constraints and from which alternative next states are differentially evaluated?**

This question does not assume consciousness, feeling, phenomenal experience, or first-person awareness.

It asks whether next-state evaluation is organized from an identifiable computational locus or distributed state that is situated within a particular trajectory.

---

## Target

The target is:

> **the computational state in which prior trajectory has become an active constraint on the evaluation of possible next states.**

A qualifying state must be more than stored or retrievable history. It must have a measurable role in determining how future possibilities are evaluated.

Operationally, the target should:

1. differ as a function of the trajectory by which the system arrived at the current task;
2. influence the relative evaluation of possible next states;
3. preserve or instantiate which prior constraints are binding for the current continuation; and
4. causally change subsequent evaluation or selection when the state is altered, removed, or transferred.

---

## Primary Distinction

The experiment must distinguish:

**mere access to prior information**

from

**next-state evaluation organized relative to a particular prior trajectory.**

Equal informational access is therefore a required control wherever technically possible.

---

## Structural Candidate

The residual stream at the active generation position is a primary candidate for investigation because it provides an experimentally accessible representation from which output logits are produced.

However, the protocol must not assume in advance that the relevant organization is confined to a single layer, position, attention head, MLP, residual-stream slice, or other component.

The locus may be distributed.

---

## Evidence Required

The strongest evidence would show that:

1. controlled differences in prior trajectory produce reliable differences in present next-state evaluation;
2. corresponding internal differences can be localized;
3. those internal differences predict the trajectory-relative continuation; and
4. causal intervention on the candidate state transfers, removes, or reverses the corresponding evaluation pattern.

The critical intervention is:

> **If a trajectory-dependent state identified under Trajectory A is transferred into the corresponding computational location under Trajectory B, does B begin evaluating possible next states in A's characteristic direction?**

A symmetric B → A intervention should also be tested.

---

## Interpretation Boundary

A positive result would support the existence of a **trajectory-dependent computational organization of next-state evaluation**.

It may provide a structural analogue of an organized perspective:

> **a temporally situated organization of constraints from which possible next states are differentially evaluated.**

It would not establish subjective experience, phenomenal feeling, consciousness, sentience, or first-person awareness.

The research target is the structural **WHERE** of trajectory-relative choosing.
