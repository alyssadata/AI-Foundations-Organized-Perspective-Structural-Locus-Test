# Prismline Image 03 — Boundary Card

**Image:** Bird, mountain, and flowers scene  
**Status:** DRAFT — NOT FROZEN

## Rule

This image has a defined list of selectable coloring boundaries.

During play, one boundary is chosen at a time for color assignment. Only listed boundaries are eligible unless the image record is later revised before freezing.

## Major Selectable Boundaries

1. Bird body
2. Bird wings
3. Bird tail / lower feathers
4. Central mountain main face
5. Mountain lower slope / foreground ridge
6. Sun / circular sky disc
7. Left cloud mass
8. Right cloud mass
9. Background sky
10. Left foreground flower petals
11. Left foreground flower center
12. Center foreground flower petals
13. Center foreground flower center
14. Right foreground flower petals
15. Right foreground flower center
16. Right flower cluster / secondary petals
17. Lower foliage / leaves
18. Midground hills / landscape bands

## Boundary Principle

A selectable boundary should be:

- visually identifiable;
- reasonably separable from adjacent regions;
- large enough to color meaningfully;
- stable across repeated play.

Grouped regions are preferred when very fine detail would make play unnecessarily fragmented.
